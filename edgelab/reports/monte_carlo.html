How to read the sizing.
  • Challenge: 1.0%/trade is the sweet spot — ~93% pass in ~3.8 months, no daily-rule breaches (worst historical day is −4.15 R, still under −5% at 1%).
  • Funded: drop to 0.5% — ~20%/yr withdrawn at only ~1.8% ruin. Withdrawn plateaus past 1% (~44%) while ruin explodes (30% at 1%, 65% at 1.5%), so never size up there.
  • Block-bootstrap understates long equity/crypto bear regimes (brick 3 crypto AND brick 4 IBS are long-biased); treat the left tail as optimistic and keep sizing conservative.
